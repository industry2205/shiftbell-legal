# ShiftBell — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: July 27, 2026_

ShiftBell ("the App", "we") is made by BORABADA. ShiftBell works without accounts and keeps your data on your device. This policy explains what the App does — and does not — collect.

## Data stored on your device
Your shift schedule, alarms, wake confirmations, streaks, and journal entries are stored only on your iPhone. Wake photos (if you use the Selfie method) are stored locally, encrypted at rest, and deliberately excluded from iCloud/iTunes backups. We run no servers and cannot see, collect, or access any of this information.

## Location and weather
If you enable the morning briefing, the App uses your approximate location to fetch local weather for your shift hours through Apple WeatherKit. Location is used only to retrieve that weather; we do not store your location or share it with anyone. You can deny or revoke location access in iOS Settings — the briefing simply omits weather.

Weather data is provided by Apple Weather. The meteorological sources behind it are listed on Apple's attribution page: [Apple Weather data sources](https://weatherkit.apple.com/legal-attribution.html).

## Purchases and subscriptions
ShiftBell Pro is optional. Payments are processed by Apple. We use RevenueCat to validate and manage your subscription entitlement. RevenueCat receives a randomly generated app-user identifier and your App Store purchase/receipt information; it does not receive your name or contact details. See Apple's and RevenueCat's privacy policies for how they handle this data.

## Camera (optional)
If you choose the Selfie wake method, the App opens the camera so you can take a wake photo as proof you were up. Photos stay on your device and are pruned automatically. Nothing is uploaded.

## Calendar (optional)
If you turn on holidays or calendar features, the App reads your device calendars to show holidays and events alongside your shifts. This is read-only, stays on your device, and can be turned off in Settings or revoked in iOS Settings.

## Notifications
The App schedules local alarms and reminders on your device. These are generated and delivered locally; no push server is involved.

## Apple Intelligence features
On supported devices, some features (such as describing a rotation in words, or the weekly reflection) use Apple Intelligence on-device. Your input is processed on your iPhone and is not sent to us.

## What we do NOT do
We do not use accounts or logins. We do not show ads. We do not use third-party analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data.

## Data retention and deletion
Because your data lives on your device, you are in control: you can reset your calendar, streak, and records in Settings, and deleting the App removes all of its local data. Subscription records are held by Apple and RevenueCat under their own policies.

## Children
ShiftBell is intended for a general audience of working adults and is not directed to children under 13.

## Changes to this policy
We may update this policy as the App changes. Material changes will be reflected here with a new date.
