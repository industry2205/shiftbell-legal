Questions, bug reports, or feature requests? Email **borabada77@gmail.com** and we'll get back to you.

When reporting a problem with an alarm, it helps to include:

- your iPhone model and iOS version,
- which wake method you use (Confirm, Quick Tap, Math, or Selfie),
- the date and time the alarm should have rung.

## Frequently asked

**My alarm didn't ring.**
On iOS 26.2 and later ShiftBell uses AlarmKit, which can ring through Silent Mode and Focus. On earlier iOS versions the alarm is delivered as a time-sensitive notification instead — please keep notifications allowed for ShiftBell and avoid Do Not Disturb during your wake window.

**Why is there no alarm on some workdays?**
ShiftBell only rings on days your schedule marks as working. Check the Calendar tab: days off, holidays (if you enabled "I'm off on holidays"), and manually painted rest days stay silent by design.

**Which features need Apple Intelligence?**
"Describe it" rotation drafting and the weekly reflection run on-device with Apple Intelligence, which requires an iPhone 15 Pro or later on iOS 26 with Apple Intelligence enabled. Everything else works on every supported iPhone (iOS 17+).

**How do I restore my purchase?**
Settings → ShiftBell Pro → Restore purchases, using the same Apple Account that made the original purchase.
